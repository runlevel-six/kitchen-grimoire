# How to find recipes

There are three ways in: search, browse, and the home page's lists. They
all end up on the same results page, where filters combine.

## Search

Type in the search box on the home page or the results page. On the
results page, results update as you type.

| To find | Type |
|---|---|
| Recipes with both words | `lemon bars` |
| Words starting with what you typed (the last word only) | `butter` (also finds *buttermilk*) |
| An exact phrase | `"cream cheese"` |
| Recipes without a word | `cookies -nuts` |
| By who it came from | `aunt` |
| By ingredient | `buttermilk` |

Search covers titles, ingredients, tags and category names, contributor,
author and collection, and the description, steps, and notes, with title
matches ranked first. Case and accents don't matter. Full rules:
[Search syntax](../reference/search-syntax.md).

If nothing matches, try fewer words, or browse.

## Browse

Tap **Browse** at the top of any page. It lists, each with a count:

- **Categories**, as a tree. A category includes its sub-categories:
  *Desserts* shows the cookies too.
- **From**: the people recipes came from.
- **Collections**, such as a family cookbook.
- **Tags**.

Tap any of them to see those recipes, or **All N recipes, A–Z** for the
whole library.

## Narrow down with filters

A browse link opens the results page with a **filter**, shown as a chip
above the results (e.g. *Category: Desserts › Cookies ✕*). Type in the
search box to search within it. Tap the chip's **✕** to remove the filter
and keep the search.

Filters combine with each other. There are no buttons for every filter;
the less common ones are reached through the address, which you can
bookmark:

| Filter | Add to `/search?` |
|---|---|
| Your favorites | `fav=1` |
| Recipes with a photo | `photo=1` |
| Flagged for review | `review=1` (or use **Review queue** in the footer) |
| A category | `cat=desserts/cookies` |
| A tag | `tag=make-ahead` |
| From someone | `by=Aunt Example` |
| A collection | `col=Example Family Cookbook` |

For example, `/search?q=chocolate&fav=1&photo=1` finds your favorites with
a photo that mention chocolate.

## From the home page

The home page shows **Your favorites**, **Recently added**, and **Recently
edited**. Tap **☆ Favorite** on a recipe to pin it there.
